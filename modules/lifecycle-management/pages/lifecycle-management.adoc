== Lifecycle Management

Lifecycle management for the Krkn-operator encompasses the administrative tasks required to maintain a stable and current chaos engineering environment within your Kubernetes cluster.

*   **Upgrades:** Performing seamless operator updates to ensure the latest features and stability patches are applied.
*   **Versioning:** Managing release cycles to maintain compatibility with your cluster environment and experiment requirements.
*   **Health Maintenance:** Monitoring operator performance and resource utilization to ensure consistent availability for orchestrating chaos scenarios.
