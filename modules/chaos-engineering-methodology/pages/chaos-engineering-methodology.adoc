#  Chaos engineering methodology

= Chaos engineering methodology

Chaos engineering is a proactive approach to identifying system weaknesses by injecting controlled disruptions. The methodology focuses on validating system resilience before failures occur in production environments.

*   **Lifecycle Management**: Follows a structured process from hypothesis generation and steady-state definition to disruption execution and recovery validation.
*   **Proactive Strategy**: Shifts from reactive incident response to proactive identification of failure modes, ensuring systems can withstand stress.
*   **Performance Evaluation**: Measures key metrics—such as latency, throughput, and error rates—against established Service Level Objectives (SLOs) during disruption.
*   **Safety and Control**: Prioritizes blast radius management and rollback planning to minimize impact on users, whether testing in staging or production environments.
*   **Observability**: Relies on robust monitoring to analyze component behavior and verify automated recovery mechanisms during and after chaos injection.
